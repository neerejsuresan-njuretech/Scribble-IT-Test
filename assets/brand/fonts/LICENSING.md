# SCRIBBLE-IT Typography Licensing Notes

SCRIBBLE-IT locally bundles open-source typography assets for consistent rendering across Web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS applications without external CDN dependencies.

## Bundled Typefaces

| Typeface | Role | Formats Included | License | License File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Inter** | Primary UI, body copy, tables, operational forms | `Inter-Variable.ttf`, `inter-latin-{400,500,600,700}-normal.woff2` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1) | `assets/brand/fonts/Inter/OFL.txt` |
| **Manrope** | Display headings, wordmark alignment, hero headlines | `Manrope-Variable.ttf`, `manrope-latin-{600,700,800}-normal.woff2` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-1.1) | `assets/brand/fonts/Manrope/OFL.txt` |

## Compliance Summary (SIL OFL 1.1)

1. **Redistribution & Bundling**: Both Inter and Manrope may be freely bundled, embedded, and redistributed inside open-source and commercial SCRIBBLE-IT desktop, mobile, and web applications.
2. **License Preservation**: The respective `OFL.txt` files must remain bundled alongside font files in source and binary distributions.
3. **No Standalone Resale**: The font files themselves may not be sold by themselves, though they may be bundled with any software distribution.
4. **No Trademark Grant**: Bundling Inter or Manrope does not imply endorsement by their respective authors.
5. **Compatibility with AGPL-3.0-only**: SIL OFL 1.1 permits aggregation/bundling alongside AGPL-3.0-only software without conflict.
